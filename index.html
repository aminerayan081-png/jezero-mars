An educational website made by a student team. Every piece of information is labelled so you can tell real data from calculations and simulations.

  • CALCULATION Worked out by us from real data, with the method shown.

About this map

A plain latitude/longitude grid (equirectangular) with 256-pixel tiles, as served by NASA Solar System Treks. It is not the Earth “Web Mercator” map. The scale is calculated with the Mars radius of 3,396,190 m (USGS metadata).

The dashed orange rectangle outlines the area covered by a USGS 6 m/pixel context-camera mosaic of Jezero. It is an outline only: it is not the crater rim, and that 6 m image is not loaded here.
