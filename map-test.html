Map geometry

Plain latitude/longitude grid (equirectangular), 256-pixel tiles, level 0 = 2 tiles wide by 1 tall, row 0 at the north. Not the Earth “Web Mercator” map. Scale is calculated with the Mars radius of 3,396,190 m (USGS metadata).

The dashed orange rectangle is the outline of a USGS 6 m/pixel context-camera mosaic of the Jezero area. It is only an outline; it is not the crater rim and the 6 m image itself is not loaded here.
