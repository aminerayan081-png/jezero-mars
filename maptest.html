    What this map shows

    Basemap: Viking MDIM 2.1 colorized global mosaic, about 232 m per pixel, made by NASA Ames and USGS, served by NASA Mars Trek. Jezero crater is only a few hundred pixels wide at this resolution, so it will look blurry when zoomed in.

    Orange rectangle: the area covered by USGS map SIM 3464 (limits taken from its metadata). The dot is the calculated centre of that rectangle, not a named feature.
